📌 Project Overview

This project presents a complete Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Power BI.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights to understand:

Sales performance
Profit trends
Customer behavior
Peak business hours
Product performance
🎯 Project Objectives

✔ Analyze overall sales and profit performance
✔ Identify top-selling coffee products
✔ Understand hourly and daily sales trends
✔ Compare payment methods (Card vs Cash)
✔ Identify peak business hours
✔ Generate actionable business insights

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
KPI	Value
💰 Total Revenue	₹115,428
📈 Total Profit	₹79,950 (69.26%)
📦 Total Transactions	4,000+
💵 Avg Transaction Value	₹31.75
👥 Unique Customers	1,000+

📈 Dashboard Features
🛍 Sales Analysis
Product-wise revenue comparison
Top-selling coffee products
Monthly and daily trends

Key Insight:
☕ Latte generated the highest revenue (₹38K+)

⏰ Time-Based Analysis
Peak hours: 8 AM – 11 AM
Highest traffic at 10–11 AM (349+ transactions)
Evening hours show low activity

Insight: Morning hours are the most profitable.

💳 Payment Analysis
Card payments: 85%+
Cash payments: 15%

Insight: Digital payments dominate customer behavior.

📅 Day-wise Performance
Tuesday → Highest sales
Friday → Strong performance
Sunday → Lowest sales

Recommendation: Run weekend promotions.

🧠 Key Business Insights
☕ Latte is the top revenue product
⏰ Morning hours drive maximum sales
📉 Sunday requires promotional strategies
💳 Card payments dominate transactions
📊 Profit margin is strong at 69.26%
